A different kind of Kariba stay

Come for the lake. Stay for the feeling.

Set above the water at Mica Point, Big 5 Lodge brings people together without making everyone stay the same way. Choose a spacious lodge with a chef, a practical self-catering cottage, or a separate family home with its own kitchen and verandah.

Gather for a swim, share a meal after a day on the water, then settle in as the sky changes over the lake. It’s a place designed around the people you bring with you—and the time you want to spend together.

Standby power

A 6.5 kVA generator backs up the municipal electricity supply during outages.

Solar hot water

Solar geysers are supported by an electric backup system for cloudy conditions.

Private, walled estate

Three separate accommodation options share landscaped grounds and the main entertainment area.

Chef & flexible catering

Main Lodge guests can choose a catered stay or self-cater with chef preparation available at no extra charge; confirm arrangements when booking.

Pool, bar & verandah

Make the shared swimming pool, outdoor bar and entertainment deck part of your day.

Housekeeping & essentials

Daily housekeeping is provided. Air conditioning and Wi-Fi are listed among the property's amenities.

Drinking water

Tap water is municipal and not recommended for drinking. Please confirm bottled or purified drinking-water arrangements with management.

Clear check-in times

Check-in from 14:00; check-out by 10:00. Ask management about flexibility before arrival.
